Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions.

Browse our shop, add products to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay with credit/debit cards, PayPal, or Apple Pay. After placing your order, you'll receive a confirmation email with your order details.

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, orders enter processing and cannot be changed. Please contact our support team immediately if you need to make changes.

We offer Standard (5-7 business days), Express (2-3 business days), and Next-Day delivery. Free standard shipping on orders over $50. Express shipping starts at $9.99. Next-day delivery is $19.99.

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. You can see shipping costs at checkout before completing your order. Note that customs duties may apply for international orders.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are encrypted and processed securely. We never store your full card details on our servers.

Absolutely. We use SSL encryption for all pages, PCI DSS compliant payment processing, and never store credit card information. Your personal data is protected under our strict privacy policy.

Go to the Returns section above, enter your order number and email, select a reason, and submit. We'll email you a prepaid return label. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorized shipping location. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days of receipt.

We offer a 30-day return policy from the date of delivery. Items must be in their original condition with all packaging. Some items like opened software, personal care products, and custom orders are final sale. Sale items can be returned for store credit.
